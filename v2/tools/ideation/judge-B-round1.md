- **Postmortem forensics:** C12 Incident Table ≈ F9 Evidence Wall. Keep C12 (table beats corkboard cliché).
- **Retro mainframe cluster:** C5 ≈ D13 ≈ F6. All cull.
- **"The Vault" name collision:** B20 (fiber splice vault) vs D19 (LTO archive). Different subjects; D19 advances, B20 doesn't.
- **Stone-record cluster:** F5 RFC Colonnade ≈ F18 Memorial Wall ≈ F10 Rosetta. All three score well but three granite monuments is one idea; if the panel wants diversity, keep F5 + F11 (cursor is terminal, not stone) and drop F18/F10.

- **Tunnel/conduit:** A18 Keepalive ≈ B10 The Tunnel. Keep B10.

## Single strongest concept

**Stackup Sediment (A3), 88.** Sixteen layers = the 16-RFC spec, expressed as geology, on one vertical axis, with the via-column pulse as the money shot. It is the only concept that fuses his hero line, his spec, and his palette into one camera direction with zero overlay fakery and a trivial asset budget (all-authored parametric geometry). Highest polish ceiling per dollar in the pool.

## Three most OVERRATED concepts

1. **The Mesh Pavilion (B3, 79)** — ideator-starred and will charm the other judge with "physical machined nodes." Doesn't matter: a suspended node-graph with a top-down "living diagram" act is the single most overused WebGL trope alive. Brass and wires can't de-trope it.
2. **The Night Desk (C1, 72)** — family C's 9/10 top pick, and indistinguishable from ten thousand r/battlestations dev portfolios. Monitor-glow + dashboards is a screen-content crutch, not a scene; fails the "could ONLY be his site" test outright.
3. **Blackwater Vent Field (E3, 68)** — ideator-starred as "highest Awwwards ceiling." Actually a BBC documentary with volumetric-smoke budget blowout, a metaphor two inferences deep, and profession drift into marine biology. The top-down topology reveal is the only keeper — and D1/B2 do topology reveals in his actual world.
