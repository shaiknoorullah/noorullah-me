# Family C — THE ENGINEER'S DEN (20 concepts)

Ideator: Family C. All Sketchfab anchors verified via `tools/sketchfab/search.py`
(server-side filtered to CC0/CC-BY + downloadable; tris counts as reported by API).
Ratings are self-assessed /10 against: relevance (king), cohesion, pillar fit, scroll-cinema potential, build risk.

### 1. The Night Desk ★ (9/10)
- **Scene:** One engineer's desk in a lightless room at 3 a.m. — ultrawide monitor glowing over a mechanical keyboard, a notebook, a cold coffee, nothing else lit.
- **Scroll story:** Acts dolly from the doorway dark → across the desk surface → macro of the terminal cursor → top-down of keyboard + notebook → crane up to the monitor wall of green dashboards → pullback to the whole room as one pool of light.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — the room is calm, ordered, unhurried; the machine does the hurrying.
- **Asset anchors:** "Modern PC Desk Setup" — Fahriyan Fikri Pradana (125k tris, decimate); "Ultrawide Monitor" — Superenforcer_xp (46k tris); "Mechanical Keyboard - Aesthetic" — M.Reslan (18k tris).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** A single room, a single desk, one light story (monitor glow + ember rim from a hallway crack).
- **Polish hook:** The macro pass across the desk surface — dust motes in the monitor glow, screen reflections crawling across the keycaps as dashboards tick.
- **Risk:** Generic "setup photo" territory — needs authored specificity (his terminal font, his RFC pinned as a prop) or it reads as any dev's desk.

### 2. The Rack ★ (9/10)
- **Scene:** A single 42U server rack standing in an otherwise black room, LEDs breathing slow green, fans audible in the grade — the substrate itself as the hero.
- **Scroll story:** Acts rise floor-to-top along the rack face → macro of blinking NIC LEDs → truck sideways into the cable-dressing side → top-down of the perforated door as a grid of light → dolly through the door into the hot aisle of blades → pullback to the lone rack in the void.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" — literally the layer everyone else rents, owned and humming in his den.
- **Asset anchors:** "Server Rack" — Shalmon (74.8k tris); "Server rack" — Osho (29.7k tris); "Network Server Rack" — Khoa Nguyen (1.1k tris).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One subject, one material system (powder-coat black steel + green emitter points), one breathing LED rhythm across the whole scene.
- **Polish hook:** The top-down of the mesh door — hundreds of status LEDs diffused through perforated steel into a living green constellation.
- **Risk:** Datacenter cliché; must read as *his* home-lab rack (hand-labeled cables, one empty U slot) not a stock colo beauty shot.

### 3. The Console of One ★ (8.5/10)
- **Scene:** A vintage control-room console rebuilt for one operator — ten small terminal tiles across its surface, each an agent working a ticket, one empty chair.
- **Scroll story:** Acts open on the empty chair → push past it to the console → macro of one ticket tile resolving itself → truck across all ten tiles working in parallel → crane up to reveal the wall dashboard: one engineer, ten threads → pullback to the single human silhouette.
- **Pillar(s):** "Leverage, not heroics" — the ten-agent platform made spatial: one seat, ten hands.
- **Asset anchors:** "Type 30 Console" — themighty808 (164k tris, decimate hard); "Large Wall-Mounted Computer Console" — Inditrion Dradnon (1.1k tris); Blender-authored tile screens.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One instrument (the console) fills the room; every screen is the same UI system, so density never becomes clutter.
- **Polish hook:** The crane-up reveal — ten tiles resolving in parallel while the chair stays empty, the room doing the work.
- **Risk:** The "ten screens" idea can read as a generic hacker wall; the restraint (one console, one UI language, one chair) is the whole game.

## Family's strongest angle

The single dark room lit only by its own instruments — monitor glow, rack LEDs, one green
signal in true black — is the family's native language, and it maps directly onto his hero
line: the den IS the substrate, and the six scroll acts can tour one room without ever
breaking cohesion. Strongest executions put *one authored subject* (his desk, his rack, his
console) at the center and let props prove the pillars instead of decorating them.
