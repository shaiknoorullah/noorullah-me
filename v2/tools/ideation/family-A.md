# Family A — SILICON WORLDS: 20 scene concepts

Person: Shaik Noorullah — platform/SRE engineer (Kubernetes PaaS, 99.999%, 16-RFC spec, GitOps, observability, 35+ agent platform). Palette: true black, bone-white, green #A4EB53 + ember rim + cool fill. One scene, 6 scroll acts, CC0/CC-BY Sketchfab + Blender, 60fps.

---

### The Substrate ★
- **Scene:** A single CPU package floats in a true-black void; the scroll dives from the engraved heatspreader through the lid into the exposed silicon die, where transistor blocks glow faint green like a waking grid.
- **Scroll story:** Slow orbit of the package → dolly to the laser-etched markings → crane as the IHS lifts away → top-down of the die floorplan → macro of a single logic block switching → pullback to the whole chip, now fully lit from within.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" — the hero line made literal: the camera goes below the layer everyone else stops at.
- **Asset anchors:** "Microchip - Prototype" by re1monsen (97,981 tris); "AMD Ryzen Threadripper" by VivernaNeva (672 tris, package shell); "CPU" by Panda (28,095 tris) — die interior Blender-authored.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One subject, one continuous zoom of five nested scales; single light story = green signal emissive rising from inside the silicon.
- **Polish hook:** The macro where a logic block's gates cascade green across the die like a city grid waking — one emissive wave, total black elsewhere.
- **Risk:** Scale-continuity across the dive (package → die → gate) is the hard part; die interior must be authored to survive the macro act without going soft.

### Ten Cores, One Conductor ★
- **Scene:** Top-down onto a multi-core processor die rendered as a dark floorplan where ten identical compute cores pulse green in sequence as work routes between them, orchestrated by one scheduler block at the center.
- **Scroll story:** Wide top-down of the die → truck along a row of cores → dolly into the scheduler block as it dispatches → macro of one core saturating then draining calm → crane up as all ten hum at steady load → pullback to the whole die breathing at one clock.
- **Pillar(s):** "Leverage, not heroics" — one conductor, ten workers: the agent platform drawn in silicon; also "Composed under load" — nothing hurries, queues never overflow.
- **Asset anchors:** "CHIPSET 001" by AGW Entertainment (165,888 tris) as floorplan reference; core array and interconnect Blender-authored instanced geometry (cheap: 10 instances of one core).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** Single subject (one die), single rhythm (one clock domain drives every emissive pulse in the scene).
- **Polish hook:** The top-down god-view where all ten cores fire in a traveling green wave, phase-locked to an audible-clock tick — a Kärlek-style "one system, one beat" moment.
- **Risk:** Too abstract without copy support; needs the ticket/agent metaphor carried by type, not by literal UI overlays that would break the scene.

### Stackup Sediment ★
- **Scene:** A single PCB's 16-layer stackup, opened like a geological core sample — copper, prepreg, ground planes and via columns revealed as dark strata with green signal traces threading vertically through all of them.
- **Scroll story:** Hero shot of the intact black board → crane as layers separate a few millimeters → dolly down through plane after plane → macro of a single via column carrying green pulses across all strata → top-down of the alignment → pullback as the 16 layers fuse back into one board.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" (the layers literally below the surface) with a quiet nod to the 16-RFC platform spec — sixteen layers, each with a purpose.
- **Asset anchors:** Blender-authored only — parametric stackup from planes + extruded trace curves + instanced via cylinders; guaranteed cohesive and light.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One board, one vertical axis; every camera act moves along that single axis, and the via columns visually stitch all layers into one object.
- **Polish hook:** The macro of one via column: a green pulse descends through all sixteen strata in a single unbroken fall, ember rim on the copper barrel — the "verticality of ownership" shot.
- **Risk:** If layers drift too far apart it becomes the banned "stitched objects" look — separation must stay tight, mechanical, almost reluctant.

### Motherboard Metropolis at 3AM
- **Scene:** A full ATX motherboard seen as a sleeping city at night — capacitors as towers, traces as streets, VRM heatsinks as downtown blocks — with slow green pulses commuting along the bus lines.
- **Scroll story:** High aerial of the board-city → descent between the capacitor towers → street-level dolly down a trace canyon → macro of a single green pulse passing under a chip → crane over the VRM district → pullback to the whole board glowing like a calm grid.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — a city that never sleeps but never hurries; traffic flows, nothing jams.
- **Asset anchors:** "MAXIMUS VI Formula" by Vivien Deroche (61,092 tris); "MotherBoard + Components" by Daniel Cardona (82,090 tris).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One board is the whole world; the city reading comes from camera height and fog, not added props.
- **Polish hook:** The street-level act: camera at trace height, capacitor monoliths towering in ember rim light while a green bus pulse passes overhead like a train.
- **Risk:** "Circuit city" is the oldest cliché in the family — survives only with total restraint: no neon, no rain, no cyberpunk; black solder mask and one green.

### Cold Aisle
- **Scene:** A single datacenter aisle in darkness — two facing rows of monolithic racks, one green status LED per chassis blinking in slow synchrony like a heartbeat.
- **Scroll story:** End-of-aisle establishing shot → slow dolly down the aisle → truck across rack faces of blinking lights → macro of one LED and its laser-etched label → crane up the rack elevations → pullback from above, the aisle a single green waveform.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — 99.999% drawn as an unhurried heartbeat; the system is calm because it was built right.
- **Asset anchors:** "Data Center Server Rack" by EntropyNine (27,853 tris); "Server Rack" by Shalmon (74,823 tris) — aisle assembled from instanced racks.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One corridor, one repeating module, one light rhythm; the sync of the LEDs is the scene's single event.
- **Polish hook:** The pullback where the whole aisle's LEDs reveal themselves as one long ECG-like green waveform — uptime as a vital sign.
- **Risk:** Relevance inversion — a datacenter can read as "the cloud he replaces," not the substrate he owns; framing must read bare-metal ownership, and dark-corridor readability needs careful emissive budgeting.

### The Five-Nines Heart
- **Scene:** A monumental quartz crystal oscillator standing in a black void like a metronome temple, its silver can half-open to show the quartz blank, the whole scene breathing at its resonant frequency.
- **Scroll story:** Wide shot of the can → orbit of the etched markings → macro of the quartz blank vibrating (visible only as light refraction) → top-down into the open can → dolly to the electrode → pullback as each oscillation sends one green clock pulse out into darkness.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — the 99.999% target as a heartbeat that never misses; one component everything else trusts.
- **Asset anchors:** "EPSON SG-3030CM CRYSTAL OSCILLATOR (SPXO)" by Caddalog (4,778 tris); interior quartz and mount Blender-authored.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One component, one frequency; every animation in the scene is phase-locked to the same period.
- **Polish hook:** The macro of the quartz blank: each cycle bends the ember rim light for a frame, then emits a single green pulse — time itself made visible.
- **Risk:** Obscurity — most viewers won't recognize the part; the "clock of the whole system" story must land in type or it reads as a random silver can.

### Electron River
- **Scene:** The interior of a single copper trace at electron scale — a dark hexagonal canyon of copper atoms where a slow green current drifts like a river under an electric field.
- **Scroll story:** Descent into the trace → drift along the current → truck past a grain boundary → macro of a single electron hopping atoms → crane over a via plunging downward → pullback revealing the trace is one line of a vast board.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" taken to its physical floor — he owns the layer all the way down to moving charge.
- **Asset anchors:** Blender-authored only — instanced atom lattice + particle flow on a curve; no Sketchfab model survives this scale.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One trace, one direction of flow; the camera never leaves the copper.
- **Polish hook:** The pullback reveal: the intimate river you've followed for five acts is one hairline trace on a black board — scale vertigo, corn-style.
- **Risk:** Drifts toward abstract tunnel/terrain (rejected family territory); the copper lattice and grain boundaries must stay visibly crystalline, not cave-like.

### Wafer World
- **Scene:** A 300mm silicon wafer lying in darkness like a dark mirror planet, its grid of dies catching iridescent diffraction while a single green inspection scan line sweeps the surface.
- **Scroll story:** Edge-on sliver of the wafer → crane over the mirror surface → top-down of the die grid → macro of one die's floorplan → truck along the scribe lines → pullback as the scan line completes and one die lights green: yield.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" — the wafer is the substrate before it becomes anyone's product; "Evidence over assertion" via the inspection scan and the one verified-good die.
- **Asset anchors:** Blender-authored only — no CC wafer model found; a disc + procedural die-grid + thin-film iridescence shader.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One object, one surface, one sweeping scan line as the only motion event.
- **Polish hook:** The top-down: bone-white scribe grid on black silicon, rainbow diffraction at grazing angles, and the green scan line arriving at a single die which answers back in green.
- **Risk:** Fully authored asset + iridescence shader is real R&D; a flat mirror disc can also read as "a CD" if the die grid doesn't read at first glance.

### BGA Forest
- **Scene:** The underside of a flipped chip package: a thousand solder balls standing as an infinite mercury forest on a black substrate, with green signal vias descending between them.
- **Scroll story:** Package flipping over → dolly across the solder-ball plane → descent into the forest → macro of one ball's meniscus catching ember rim → crane through the grid at ball height → pullback revealing the full package as a held artifact.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" — the connection layer nobody sees, the interface where the chip meets the world it serves.
- **Asset anchors:** Blender-authored only — instanced spheres with surface-tension shading over a substrate plane (trivially cheap at 60fps).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One package, one grid; the repetition is the subject, so more instances only strengthen it.
- **Polish hook:** The ball-height crane shot: a thousand perfect silver menisci stretching to a black horizon, each catching the same ember rim at a different angle — Rothko made of solder.
- **Risk:** Monotony — a pure repetition piece lives or dies on lighting; without the ember/green interplay it's a screensaver.

### The Power Plant
- **Scene:** A VRM district rendered monumental: rows of chokes and capacitors as an industrial power station on black solder mask, feeding invisible current to a distant glowing die.
- **Scroll story:** Wide of the VRM array → truck down the choke row → dolly through the capacitor tanks → macro of a single choke's copper winding with ember rim → crane over the phase controllers → pullback showing the power planes converging on the socket.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" — power delivery is the layer beneath compute; "Composed under load" — sixteen phases sharing load evenly, nothing straining.
- **Asset anchors:** "MAXIMUS VI Formula" by Vivien Deroche (61,092 tris, VRM section); "Computer Components" by crimsonfalcon (36,777 tris).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One board region, one material system (ferrite, copper, polymer caps), one flow direction: everything points at the socket.
- **Polish hook:** The choke macro: hair-thin copper windings catching ember light while a green current ripple travels the coil — industry at millimeter scale.
- **Risk:** Readability — VRMs are the least glamorous parts on a board; the "power station" reading depends entirely on camera scale and fog.

### Switch Fabric
- **Scene:** The interior of a network switch ASIC: a dark crossbar of lanes where green packet cells stream between ports, never colliding, scheduled in perfect silence.
- **Scroll story:** Exterior of the black 1U switch → camera passes through the faceplate between the SFP cages → top-down of the crossbar fabric → macro of one cell switching lanes → truck alongside a full-rate stream → pullback as all ports run line-rate, calm.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — line rate with zero drops as the definition of calm; quiet nod to his Cilium/WireGuard networking world.
- **Asset anchors:** "TL-SF1005D" by arieffitrah (26,300 tris, exterior); "Network Switching" by hjhj87 (26,639 tris); fabric interior Blender-authored.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One device, one fabric; the exterior-to-interior pass-through is a single unbroken camera move.
- **Polish hook:** The top-down crossbar: a green grid of orthogonal streams that weave through each other at the intersections without ever touching — choreography as engineering.
- **Risk:** The interior is invented geometry; if it looks like a generic "data tunnel" it falls into cliché — must stay rectilinear, clocked, packetized.

### The NAND Vault
- **Scene:** A single M.2 NVMe stick standing vertically like a black monolith skyline, its NAND packages as sealed archive vaults, one green activity LED pulsing as data is committed.
- **Scroll story:** Low-angle hero of the standing stick → crane up the monolith → dolly across the NAND packages → macro of one package's etched markings → top-down of the stick's gold edge connector → pullback as a write completes and the LED settles to idle.
- **Pillar(s):** "Evidence over assertion" — the vault where commits, PRs and RFCs physically live; the pitch is stored, not claimed.
- **Asset anchors:** "NVME SSD 2280" by TDD (19,956 tris); "Samsung 980 Pro NVMe 1tb" by BlenderFace (1,392 tris).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One artifact, one orientation; treating the stick as architecture keeps every act on the same subject.
- **Polish hook:** The low-angle opening: a 22×80mm stick shot like the monolith from 2001, bone-white silkscreen reading in ember rim — commodity hardware made monumental.
- **Risk:** Product-shot drift — an SSD is a catalog object; without the vault/commit narrative it reads as an ad for Samsung.

### Boot Sequence
- **Scene:** A single motherboard on a black bench at the moment of power-on: PSU rails ramp, status LEDs strike in strict sequence, fans spool, and the board comes alive in total darkness.
- **Scroll story:** Black frame, one standby LED → dolly as the 24-pin energizes → truck across power stages lighting in order → macro of the first green POST LED striking → crane as fans spin up and the DRAM training lights ripple → pullback to the fully booted board, steady state.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — determinism as calm: every rail arrives exactly when it should; also "Evidence" — POST codes as observable truth.
- **Asset anchors:** "MotherBoard + Components" by Daniel Cardona (82,090 tris); "PSU Power Supply Unit" by Groovex (12,488 tris, edge of frame only).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One board, one event (power-on); the scroll acts are the boot order, so narrative and cinematography are the same timeline.
- **Polish hook:** Act one: five seconds of pure black with a single amber standby LED — then the first green strike. Restraint as spectacle.
- **Risk:** The early acts are nearly empty frames; pacing must be musical or the first two scroll screens feel broken rather than restrained.

### Thermal Forensics
- **Scene:** A motherboard viewed through a thermal-imaging gradient: most of the board cold black-blue, one ember-hot spot blooming — and the camera investigates it like a detective down to a single failing MOSFET.
- **Scroll story:** Full-board thermal overview → dolly toward the hot bloom → truck across the heat gradient spreading through the planes → macro of the single incandescent component → top-down of the heat plume → pullback as the component is isolated, annotated in bone-white.
- **Pillar(s):** "Evidence over assertion" — incident forensics made literal: you don't argue about the outage, you look at the heat.
- **Asset anchors:** "MAXIMUS VI Formula" by Vivien Deroche (61,092 tris) with a custom thermal-gradient shader; the failing FET highlighted in Blender.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One board, one phenomenon (heat), one false-color material system applied to everything.
- **Polish hook:** The macro where the ember bloom resolves from an abstract gradient into an actual 3×3mm component — the moment data becomes a culprit.
- **Risk:** Palette discipline — thermal gradients naturally want rainbow; the grade must stay within black→cool→ember with green reserved for the annotation layer.

### Fin Cathedral
- **Scene:** The interior of a tower CPU cooler's fin stack as a vast ribbed cathedral: heat pipes as columns, fins as nave walls, slow dust motes drifting in a laminar airflow.
- **Scroll story:** Exterior of the cooler → camera enters between the fins → dolly down the central nave → macro of a heat pipe's sintered copper wick → crane up the fin stack → pullback as the fan turns once, slow and certain.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — cooling is composure engineered in metal: heat leaves silently, nothing throttles, nothing hurries.
- **Asset anchors:** "(Free) AMD Wraith Stealth CPU Cooler" by PolyDavid (37,975 tris); "AMD Wraith Stealth CPU Cooler" by PolyDavid (48,875 tris, alt mesh) — interior detailing in Blender.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One cooler, one airflow path; the camera follows the same path the heat takes.
- **Polish hook:** The nave shot: a hundred aluminum fins repeating into black, each catching a sliver of ember rim, one dust mote crossing a green slit of light — sacred scale from a $30 part.
- **Risk:** Subject drift — rendered too well it becomes architecture photography and the silicon story evaporates; the heat pipes must stay visibly thermal, not decorative.

### Eye Diagram
- **Scene:** A macro of a differential SerDes trace pair on black substrate where superimposed green waveforms accumulate in the air above the traces, slowly opening a luminous eye diagram.
- **Scroll story:** Dolly along the trace pair → truck as the first waveforms lift off the copper → macro of the crossing jitter → top-down of the forming eye → crane as the eye opens fully → pullback revealing the scope-less measurement happening inside the board itself.
- **Pillar(s):** "Evidence over assertion" — signal integrity as measured truth: the eye is either open or it isn't.
- **Asset anchors:** "Tektronix RM565 Oscilloscope" by Thusko (106,631 tris, optional framing prop); traces and waveforms Blender-authored shader work.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One trace pair, one measurement; the entire scene is a single signal being observed.
- **Polish hook:** The top-down of the eye: thousands of green traces stacking into one bright open eye hovering over black copper — oscilloscope art without the oscilloscope.
- **Risk:** Abstraction ceiling — viewers without EE background see pretty lines; the "measurement = truth" meaning needs one bone-white caption, no more.

### The Exposure
- **Scene:** A photomask hovering over a silicon wafer in a dark fab: one exposure field fires, and a reticle pattern prints in green onto the wafer below — a chip being born in a single flash.
- **Scroll story:** Wide of the mask suspended over the wafer → crane around the alignment marks → dolly through the gap between mask and wafer → macro of the reticle pattern → top-down at the instant of exposure → pullback as the wafer steps to the next field.
- **Pillar(s):** "Substrate over rent" — the deepest layer of all: the moment pattern becomes silicon, before any product exists.
- **Asset anchors:** Blender-authored only — mask glass, wafer disc, stepper implied by staging, never shown.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** Two parallel planes and one flash of exposure; every act stays between or around those planes.
- **Polish hook:** The exposure instant: one frame of green pattern transfer, the only bright event in six acts of darkness — lithography as a lightning strike.
- **Risk:** Adjacent to the rejected light/lamp metaphor family — the flash is process, not metaphor, but the convergence reviewers may not split that hair.

### Keepalive
- **Scene:** A single fiber run: from an SFP cage, through conduit, across a black void, into a distant rack — the camera travels its full length as green keepalive pulses pass at intervals like a heartbeat through a vein.
- **Scroll story:** Macro of the SFP latch → dolly into the connector → long travel down the fiber → truck past a splice point → crane up to the distant rack's port → pullback showing the entire span, two endpoints, one pulse mid-journey.
- **Pillar(s):** "Composed under load" — the tunnel that never sleeps and never panics; a quiet nod to WireGuard: one encrypted path, always up.
- **Asset anchors:** "Fiber-optic" by toniflutter (145,158 tris); "Submarine fiber optic cable network" by Advanced Visualization Lab (20,052 tris).
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One cable, unbroken; the scroll is a single continuous journey with no cuts.
- **Polish hook:** The mid-span shot: total black, and a single green pulse travels past the camera down the fiber's core, lighting the cladding from within for one second.
- **Risk:** Tunnel-travel cliché — a camera flying down a cable is a stock motion-graphics move; survives only if the pacing is slow and the span feels real, not hyperspace.

### Greenfield
- **Scene:** A single empty server rack in darkness that assembles itself: rails slide in, nodes seat, cables dress into perfect looms, labels appear — declarative infrastructure made physical.
- **Scroll story:** The empty rack → rails arriving → nodes seating one by one → cable looms dressing themselves → macro of a printed label → pullback to the finished rack, one green LED bar sweeping: converged.
- **Pillar(s):** "Leverage, not heroics" — GitOps/ArgoCD as self-assembly: desired state declared, reality converges, no hands.
- **Asset anchors:** "Server Rack" by Shalmon (74,823 tris); "Server V2 +console" by FlevasGR (1,104 tris) — assembly animation rigged in Blender.
- **Cohesion mechanism:** One rack, one transformation; the scene's event is convergence, and every act is a stage of it.
- **Polish hook:** The cable-dressing act: a dozen green-sleeved cables sweeping into a perfect loom in one choreographed motion — automation as ballet.
- **Risk:** Animation budget — self-assembly means rigging every component; scope creep toward a product-demo feel is the failure mode, and it must stay ritual, not advertisement.

## Self-rating & top 3

Strongest three: **The Substrate**, **Stackup Sediment**, **Ten Cores, One Conductor**.

Runners-up: Motherboard Metropolis at 3AM (proven visual language, highest cliché risk), Boot Sequence (best restraint piece, pacing risk), Thermal Forensics (best pillar-4 expression, palette risk).

**Family's strongest angle:** literalizing the hero line — go *below* the surface everyone else renders. The concepts that descend (die interior, layer stackup, vias, electron current) express "I build the substrate everyone else rents" as a camera move rather than a caption, which is exactly what a 6-act scroll piece needs: one subject, one direction, deeper every act.
